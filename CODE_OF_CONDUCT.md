# Code of Conduct

We want Team Memory to be a place where people can contribute without being harassed or demeaned.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith; disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Respect others' time, privacy and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or unwelcome attention after being asked to stop.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to the project's repositories, issues, pull requests and discussions, and to people representing the project in public. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate it and may restrict participation.

Report concerns by email to `tyler@lafronz.com` with "team-memory conduct" in the subject. Reports are handled privately where possible.
